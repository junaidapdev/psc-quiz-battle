# PSC Quiz Battle

## Idea
A quick daily quiz for Kerala PSC aspirants. On the phone, in Malayalam, ten minutes a day.

## Who it is for
Someone preparing for Kerala PSC exams like LDC and LGS, practising on their phone between work and travel.

## Mission 1 — the quiz game (build now)
1. **Start screen:** the app name, one line about it, a Start button.
2. **A round** is 10 questions picked at random from the question bank.
3. **Question screen:** one question at a time, four options as big buttons, a 20-second timer, a progress bar ("3 / 10"). Tapping an option, or the timer reaching 0, moves to the next question.
4. **Result screen:** score out of 10. Every wrong or missed question with my answer, the correct answer and a one-line explanation. A Try again button that starts a new round in a new order.
5. **Question bank** in Malayalam, five topics: Kerala history, Kerala geography, Indian Constitution, general science, Malayalam literature.

## Done means (acceptance tests)
- [ ] Double-clicking `index.html` opens the quiz in the browser. Nothing to install, no server.
- [ ] Every question has four options and exactly one correct answer.
- [ ] Every question has a one-line explanation and a source I can check myself (a textbook chapter or an official website).
- [ ] When the timer reaches 0, the quiz moves on and counts that question as wrong.
- [ ] The score on the result screen equals the number of correct answers.
- [ ] Everything fits a 360-pixel-wide phone screen, with no sideways scrolling.
- [ ] Malayalam text is easy to read and nothing is cut off.

## Mission 2 — battle mode (later, not now)
Login with Google, save every attempt, daily streaks, today's top 10, "my weak topics", a "challenge your friend" link.

## Not in this app
No payments, no ads, no admin panel. No current-affairs questions: they go out of date.
